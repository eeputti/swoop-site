swoop

Your MacBook notch, put to work.

swoop turns the notch into a file shelf, a music player and a row of widgets that you arrange and color yourself. Move the pointer to the notch and it opens. Move it away and it closes.

Free on the Mac App Store. Coming soon.

File shelf

  • Drag files to the notch to keep them on the shelf, then drag them out into any app or folder.
  • Double-click to open a file, right-click for more.
  • Files stay where they are: swoop only remembers where they are and copies nothing.
  • Drop files on the AirDrop tile, or click it to send the whole shelf to a nearby iPhone, iPad or Mac.

Music

  • Works with Spotify and Apple Music: see the song, cover and progress, and play, pause or skip right from the notch.
  • While a song plays, its cover and moving bars sit beside the camera in the closed notch.

Your notch, your way

  • A compact view that opens when you hover, and a wide view for everything else.
  • Choose the widgets for each view, put them in any order and give each one its own color.
  • Black to blend in with the notch, or glass.

Widgets

  • Timers: pomodoro, countdown and stopwatch.
  • Calendar and reminders from every account on your Mac.
  • Quick links to your apps and Notion pages, and a small prompt box for Claude or ChatGPT.
  • Battery, network speed and clock.
  • Camera mirror for a quick look before a call.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your files, camera image and calendar stay on your Mac. Read the privacy policy.

No notch? No problem.

On an external display or a Mac without a notch, swoop shows a small notch of its own at the top of the screen. Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
